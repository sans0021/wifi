Tarun Pandey, a former Indian Navy officer, transitioned from his military career to become an internet service provider with a vision to connect the world. With a deep understanding of technology and communication, he aims to bridge digital divides by providing reliable and affordable internet services. Driven by the belief that connectivity is key to progress, he strives to ensure that even the most remote areas can access the opportunities the internet brings, empowering people globally and fostering inclusive growth. His journey from the Navy to the tech world showcases his commitment to serving and innovating for a connected future.

RailTel Corporation of India Ltd. is a "Mini Ratna" Central Public Sector Enterprise (CPSE) under the Ministry of Railways, Government of India. Established in 2000, RailTel is one of the largest neutral telecom infrastructure providers in India, offering broadband and VPN services across the country.
